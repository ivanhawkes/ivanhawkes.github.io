{{/* Content card: the framed summary of one page, used by every listing. One partial, one frame: `be-best/assets/css/cards.css` styles `.card` and the `.card-list` a section lays the cards out in, so the design of a card is not repeated per content type. A card reads its own bundle for the featured image: a card with no image of its own is text only, and never the image of the section it belongs to. Callers pass a dict: page the page the card describes; omit for a card built from site data title the heading, defaulting to the page title url where the title and the image link; omit for a card that links nowhere meta one short line under the title, a position or a byline summary the body text A card is a summary of a page, so its title is an `h3`, one level under the `h1` the page carries, and is a link: `cards.css` gives it the heading ink at rest and the link colour, the underline and the focus ring on hover. The featured image is sized for the grid, not the page. `.card-list` is one column below 768px, two to 1280px and three above, with a 1rem column gap, inside a `main` gutter of `min(30px, 7%)` and a `.container` of `min(2em, 10%)` padding and a 1px border. At 1280px that leaves 372px for a card image, 744px at 2x density, so the hint names those two breakpoints and the ladder stops at 960: a card is never full width, and the 1280, 1600 and 1920 variants of a featured image are never fetched for one.
