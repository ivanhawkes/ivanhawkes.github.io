About 40 pages are under 300 words, and recipes say little of their own.

<!--more-->

## Findings

Measured on the live site on 2026-11-27, words in `<main>`: vinaigrette 103,
porchetta 115, Caesar dressing 123, garlic and ginger paste 125, bin bhuna hua
garam masala 132, Windows 10 moving in 132, the REST API design handbook 134,
Sunset Oasis 173, among about 40 under 300. Recipes are a crowded subject, and
search favours pages that show first-hand experience.

## Done When

- Each short recipe has a `notes.md` sidecar with what is the owner's own: why
  this version, what went wrong, substitutions, storage. An adapted recipe names
  its source and what was changed.
- Each old one-paragraph post is expanded, merged into a related post, or made
  noindex.
- Copies of the older posts on any earlier domain are checked for: a live copy
  elsewhere is taken as the original.
