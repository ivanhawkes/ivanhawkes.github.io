## Findings

A new domain with no inbound links is the usual reason pages sit at
"Discovered - currently not indexed". Nothing in the repository can fix it.

## Done When

- After the sitemap card is done, the sitemap is resubmitted and URL Inspection
  asks for indexing of the home page and the ten best posts.
- The GitHub, X and YouTube profiles link to `https://blog.ivanhawkes.dev/`.
- Search Console's page indexing report is read again four weeks later, and the
  result recorded here.
