This classic Welsh rarebit recipe combines the richness of melted cheddar cheese with a tangy mustard sauce, creating a delicious and satisfying dish. The bread is toasted to golden perfection and topped with the creamy sauce before being broiled until bubbly and golden brown. This hearty dish has a comforting taste that's perfect for any meal.

<!--more-->

## Directions

- Toast the bread: Preheat your oven's broiler (or grill) to high. Lightly toast the bread slices on both sides until golden and firm. Place them on a baking sheet.

- Make the roux: Melt the butter in a saucepan over medium-low heat. Stir in the flour and mustard powder. Cook for about 1 minute to form a smooth paste.

- Add the liquid: Gradually pour in the ale and Worcestershire sauce while whisking constantly to prevent lumps. Stir in the prepared English mustard and a pinch of cayenne pepper.

- Melt the cheese: Reduce the heat to low. Add the grated cheddar cheese in handfuls. Stir continuously until the cheese melts completely and you have a thick, smooth sauce. Season with pepper.

- Broil and serve: Spoon the hot cheese sauce generously over the toasted bread, spreading it right to the edges (this stops the crusts from burning). Place under the broiler for 3 to 4 minutes until the top is bubbling and golden brown. Serve immediately while hot!


## Optional

- Finely dice a small onion and add to the mix.
