This classic Welsh rarebit recipe combines the richness of melted cheddar cheese
with a tangy mustard sauce, creating a delicious and satisfying dish. The bread
is toasted to golden perfection and topped with the creamy sauce before being
broiled until bubbly and golden brown. This hearty dish has a comforting taste
that's perfect for any meal.
