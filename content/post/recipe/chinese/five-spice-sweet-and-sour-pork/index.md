A Chinese, Cantonese-style takeaway dish from the Far East: cubes of pork neck
deep-fried crisp and then glazed in a glossy sweet and sour sauce that is
sharply sweet, salty and tangy, perfumed with five-spice, Shaoxing wine and
ginger, and cut by the brightness of fresh pineapple, capsicum, tomato and
spring onion.
