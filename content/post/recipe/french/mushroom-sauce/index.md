A rich cream and butter sauce infused with earthy mushroom fragrence.

This pairs very well with steak and chips.

<!--more-->

## Mushroom Sauce

- Melt the butter in the saucepan with the garlic and shallots. Fry on a low
  heat for 2 minutes.

- Add 100ml of water and stir. The water and butter emulsion will absorb into
  the mushrooms, keeping them from becoming too greasy.

- Add the mushrooms. Fry until the water boils off, leaving just the butter and
  aromatics.

- Add the pepper, beef stock and worcestershire sauce to the pan. Cook off for a
  few minutes.

- Add the brandy and flame off.

- Add the cream. Bring to a mild simmer and allow it to reduce until it thickens
  (approximately 10-15 minutes).

## Notes

The stock I use is quite salty, so this recipe does not call for extra salt.
Season it with more salt to taste if required.
