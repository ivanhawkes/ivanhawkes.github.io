## Notes

If freezing, leave at least 1-inch head space, allowing enough room for the
liquid stock to expand as it freezes solid. (Otherwise, the expanding ice stock
will break the jar.)

Let the stock cool in the sealed jars completely before freezing. Stock should
last a week or so in the fridge, and several months in the freezer.
