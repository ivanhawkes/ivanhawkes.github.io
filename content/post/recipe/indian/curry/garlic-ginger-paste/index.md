A simple paste designed to save you time when preparing Indian dishes.

<!--more-->

## Directions

- Chop up the garlic and ginger.

- Blend it into a paste using your food processor. Add water as needed to ensure
  a smooth paste.

- Split it into 100g portions and add each to a freezer safe container. You
  should have close to 8 portions, so you might need to put one portion aside
  for tonight's dinner.
