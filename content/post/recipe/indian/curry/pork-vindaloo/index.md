## Notes

I substituted 6 tablespoons of kashmiri chilli powder for the chillies. It was
quite hot, but not uncomfortably so. You will need to determine the right amount
of chilli for your tastes.

I recommend cooking this for 20 minutes using a pressure cooker instead of the
method shown above. I will test this next time and amend the recipe if the
results are better.

You might want to serve this with a cooling yoghurt side dish to help cool the
mouth.
