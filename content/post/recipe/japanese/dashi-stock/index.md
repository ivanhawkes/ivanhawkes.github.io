## Ichiban Dashi

Makes 4 cups.

Sources disagree on whether ichiban dashi can be frozen. Some say that it can be
frozen, while others argue that the stock loses its aroma / flavor when frozen,
so it’s best to use it when it’s fresh. It’s up to you!
